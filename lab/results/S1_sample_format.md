# S1: sample format [sim]

NTSC, astronaut picture, 3 fields per run, 2 seeds per point (independent noise, start line, carrier phase). C/N per 20 MHz channel (C5VRX definition). Full reference decode: DC removal, exact polar discriminator with the wrap point in the empty part of the spectrum, wrap/click repair, digital channel filter at >= 20 MS/s, video low-pass, sync, TBC to the 910-sample grid. Gain policy: contiguous lanes and angle8 at RMS 0.45 of the +-512 window; folded lanes (4x4fine, 4x4ultra) at 4.2 cells (C5VRX ultrafine policy). I DC offset 20 ADC units.

Columns: C/N where luma SINAD reaches 10 dB (usable) and 20 dB (good), C/N where line breakup (line RMS error > 10 IRE or line missing) falls to 1 %, the noise-free ceiling (SINAD at 60 dB C/N). Median over seeds, (min..max) in brackets. Lower C/N is better.
